Our Story

Why we built this

I’ve spent the last decade in front-line outbound sales. First as a top-ranked BDR at Indeed.com, then as an Account Manager at Google Workspace, where I averaged 156% of quota for ten consecutive quarters.

What I saw across thousands of conversations was a pattern. Staffing and recruitment agencies were losing placements not because their candidates were bad, but because their business development was slow. By the time an agency’s team manually found a job posting, identified the hiring manager, and crafted an outreach, two or three competitors had already pitched their roster.

Generic B2B lead-gen tools couldn’t fix this. They weren’t built for the cadence of staffing. Job orders move in hours, not weeks. Hiring managers respond to specificity, not pattern-matched cold pitches. And no off-the-shelf platform respected the territory dynamics that make staffing competitive.

“The best candidate doesn’t win the placement. The fastest first conversation does.”

So I rebuilt my outreach methodology from the ground up, this time engineered around the live data pipeline that staffing agencies actually need: job-board scanning every thirty minutes, verified decision-maker enrichment, automated outreach that fires within minutes of a posting going live, and territory exclusivity that protects every partner agency’s competitive moat.

That’s Scale Leads Hub. Same outreach methodology that hit 156% quota at Google. Rebuilt specifically for staffing. Operated as a partner, not a vendor.
